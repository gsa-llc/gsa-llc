About GSA LLC

GSA LLC is an Iowa limited liability company based in Ames, Iowa. It is owned and run by Ryan Gibson.

What we do

Software and tools for truckers and RV owners. We make RigLedger: the RigLedger IFTA phone app (coming soon to the App Store and Google Play), spreadsheet workbooks for cost per mile, maintenance and IFTA fuel tax, and a free IFTA calculator.

Online retail. We sell products online, and brands can open a wholesale account with us.

How we build

We design the RigLedger workbooks and the app, and we use AI tools to help draft and build them. Every workbook formula is run through hand-checked scenarios before a file is listed, and the app's tax math is run through the same hand-checked IFTA test scenarios as our workbook.
