What we make

  • RigLedger IFTA app

    A phone app for iPhone and Android that logs state-line crossings, fuel purchases and odometer readings and works out the quarterly IFTA fuel tax return. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

    About the app

  • RigLedger workbooks

    Spreadsheets for Google Sheets and Excel: cost per mile, load profit and IFTA fuel tax by state for owner-operators, and maintenance and trip records for RV owners. Formulas only, no macros.

    About the workbooks

  • Free IFTA calculator

    Enter miles and gallons by state to see your fleet MPG, the tax or credit for every state, and the total for your return. No sign-up.
