RigLedger

RigLedger is GSA LLC's line of record-keeping tools for owner-operator truckers and RV owners: a fuel tax app for the phone, spreadsheet workbooks, and a free IFTA calculator. All of it lives at rigledger.gsa-company.com.

RigLedger IFTA app

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

An IFTA fuel tax log for iPhone and Android, built for the driver on a phone. It logs state-line crossings, fuel purchases and odometer readings, and works out the quarterly IFTA fuel tax return.

  • State line

    One tap when you cross. The app saves the crossing and names the new state from one location reading, checked on the phone against state lines built into the app. No signal needed.

  • Fuel

    When you're parked: snap the receipt, type the gallons, and the state is filled in. Reefer fuel can be kept out of the return with one switch.

  • Odometer

    When you're parked: type the reading. Your state miles come from your odometer, not from GPS tracking that drops miles.

  • One tap while moving. Typing waits until you park: the number pad appears only on the Fuel and Odometer screens.
  • Already have your totals from an ELD report or on paper? Type my totals takes each state's miles and tax-paid gallons and works out the return.
  • Carries the official IFTA diesel rates from IFTA, Inc.'s tax matrix and picks up each new quarter's rates free.
  • Works offline, with no account and no sign-in. Your entries, photos and settings stay on your phone.
  • Free: logging, totals, fleet MPG, your net tax due or credit, the due date and deadline reminders, and backup and restore. A one-time unlock, with no subscription, adds the per-state return lines, an audit PDF and CSV files for the RigLedger owner-operator workbook.

Built for US-based owner-operators who file their own IFTA return: one truck, diesel. It does not file the return for you, track GPS in the background, run a fleet, handle Canada-based filers, or cover gasoline or other fuels.

RigLedger workbooks

Working spreadsheets for Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel (.xlsx). Formulas only: no macros, no add-ons, no account to create with us. Every formula is run through hand-checked scenarios before a file is listed, and the pictures below are the real files.

Owner-Operator Business Workbook

Your real cost per mile, the rate you need at your target margin, the profit on each load, and IFTA fuel tax by state. Tabs include Loads, a Fuel Log with fill-to-fill MPG, IFTA by jurisdiction with the Kentucky and Virginia surcharges handled, Cost Per Mile, a Monthly P&L, and a compliance calendar for 2290, IFTA and UCR dates.

For one-truck and small-fleet owner-operators, lease-purchase drivers and hotshot haulers.

For travel trailers, fifth wheels, toy haulers, pop-ups and motorhomes.
