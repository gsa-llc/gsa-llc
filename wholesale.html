Wholesale accounts for brands

GSA LLC is a small online retailer in Ames, Iowa. We have listings on eBay today and are building our Amazon store, where we keep stock in Amazon's warehouses (FBA). We're adding brands in tools, outdoor, kitchen, home and automotive.

For brands

If you open a wholesale account with us, we will:

  • Sell at or above your MAP and follow your reseller policy
  • Sell only on your existing Amazon listings, never create duplicates or change your content
  • Prepay our orders
  • Reorder on a steady schedule

Our Iowa resale certificate and W-9 are available on request.
